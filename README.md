## 🧠 About Me

I build production-grade AI systems powered by Large Language Models, retrieval pipelines, and agentic architectures.

My work combines:
- ⚙️ AI Engineering (LLMs, APIs, scalable systems)
- 🧠 AI Strategy (enterprise AI transformation)
- 🛡️ Responsible AI (governance, ethics, risk)

I focus on building AI that is not just powerful, but also scalable, explainable, and trusted in real-world environments.

## ⚡ What I Build

- 🤖 LLM-powered applications, copilots, and assistants
- 📚 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems
- ⚙️ Agentic AI workflows and automation platforms
- 📊 AI-powered decision intelligence solutions
- 🛡️ AI governance and risk-aware AI systems
